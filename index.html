Privacy Policy for Simple Post Resizer

Last updated: September 20, 2026

ZODELABS ("we", "our", or "us") developed Simple Post Resizer as an image resizing application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

Simple Post Resizer does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

Photos and Images

The app allows you to select images from your device so that they can be resized or processed according to the options you choose.

Images selected for processing are handled on your device. We do not upload your images to our servers or collect copies of your images.

Device Permissions

The app may request access to photos or media on your device when you choose an image to resize or when you save an exported image.

These permissions are used only to provide the functionality of the application.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Data Security

Because image processing is performed on your device, your selected images are not transmitted to our servers by the app.

Children's Privacy

Simple Post Resizer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

Simple Post Resizer does not use third-party services to collect personal information from users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app, you can contact ZODELABS through the contact information provided on our official website or app listing.
